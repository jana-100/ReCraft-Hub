About Us

Meet Our Mission

ReCraft Hub is a student-run creative upcycling club dedicated to transforming discarded everyday materials into useful and beautiful new products.

Our mission is to reduce waste, promote sustainable habits, and help members develop practical skills through creative workshops and activities. We provide a welcoming community where people can learn, share ideas, and express their creativity.
